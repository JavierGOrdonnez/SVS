Violence Statistics · Spain

Reported, convicted, and survey-estimated violence against women in Spain — feminicides, sexual crime, hate crime, and the demographic baselines behind lifetime-risk estimates. Every figure carries a provenance tier; nothing marked unverified enters the models.

Migration & cohorts

Immigration flows and foreign-national stock (Eurostat / INE, 2000–2025), plus the Morocco/Algeria cohort-tenure crime-rate tests. The cohort tests are descriptive and associative — not causal.

Cohort-tenure tests · Morocco / Algeria

Poisson two-sample tests on MIR crime counts against Eurostat population denominators — does the post-2022 rise concentrate in newly-arrived cohorts? Hover a bar for its p-value and hypothesis call.

Regularization-denominator sensitivity · upper bound, not an estimate

If a nationality's ENTIRE 2026 regularization-application pool had already been resident every year 2019-2024, all aged 15-59 — the most generous denominator-undercount correction possible — how much would the rate fall? Morocco, Algeria, Colombia, Venezuela, 2024.

Long-run crime trends · Spanish vs. foreign, 2015-2023

Extends this dashboard's crime coverage back before the sexual-crimes-specific series (2019+) using MIR Anuario's general "Seguridad Ciudadana" chapter — homicide, robbery, and sexual assault. Spanish-vs-foreign only: no per-country breakdown exists in this source (checked directly, see reports/algeria_morocco_divergence.md's H1/H2 sections for where per-country data does and doesn't exist).

Hate & xenophobic crime

MIR hate-crime reports, 2016–2023. There is no 2022 edition (publication gap), and a 2017 methodology change makes the disability series discontinuous — both flagged on the charts. Nationality of detainees/investigated and victims, 2021–2024, comes from a separate source: the Interior Ministry's own statistical portal, not the PDF reports.

Mortality baseline

All-cause and external-cause mortality (INE ECM, 2000–2024) — the competing-risk denominators behind any lifetime-probability estimate.
