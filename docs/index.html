Critical Data Caveats

  • Series break 2022: LO 10/2022 (Solo sí es sí) merged "abuso sexual" into "agresión sexual" — pre/post figures are incompatible.
  • Pre-2012 MIR: Excluded Cataluña, País Vasco, Navarra (~25–30% population) — multiply by ~1.3–1.5 for national estimates.
  • Blocker B6: Two incompatible rape series in MIR data (Anuario ~5,000 vs Informe subcategory ~1,200). Root cause unresolved — blocks rate computation.
  • Survey ≠ Police: Macroencuesta captures actual prevalence; police data captures reported crimes. Don't combine as a "dark figure multiplier".
  • Reporting rate: ~8% of sexual violence outside partner is reported (Macroencuesta 2019).
  • Femicide scope: Delegación counts partner/ex-partner only. Total female homicides are higher (INE MNP).
  • 2024 Macroencuesta: Methodology change — observed differences may reflect measurement, not real change.
  • Foreign victim rate: 4.95× femicide rate ratio reflects rates per 100k, not absolute counts.
  • Haiku audit: Prior-AI values were cross-checked: femicide 2024 (58→47), total sexual crimes 2024 (21,159→22,846), 2023 total (19,981→21,825).

Migration Data Caveats

  • EVR → EM break (2008): Methodology source changes; pre-2008 not strictly comparable to 2008+.
  • EM → EMCR revision (2021): 2021 figure rises from 528,856 → 887,960 after revision. Treat as a methodology step.
  • Padrón → ECP (2021): Stock methodology changes 1 Jan 2021. Provisional vs definitivo vintages differ.
  • Brexit (2020): UK citizens reclassified from EU to non-EU in flow tables from January 2020.
  • "Country of origin" ≠ "country of nationality" ≠ "country of birth": Three distinct variables in INE tables — match the question to the right variable.
  • No occupation data in EMCR: MISSM affiliation is a proxy and only captures workers with Social Security coverage (excludes informal sector and short-stay arrivals).
  • Foreign-born ≠ foreign-national: The stock series for "foreign-born" includes naturalised Spanish citizens; counts diverge over time as naturalisations accumulate.
