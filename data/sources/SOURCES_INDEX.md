# Sources Index

One-page map over the living source documents in `data/sources/`. Each entry: what it covers,
the confidence level of its key figures, and the caveats most likely to bite when joining or
comparing series. For verification workflow and spot-check values, see `../../VERIFICATION_GUIDE.md`.

| Doc | Scope | Confidence of key figures | Known issues to watch |
|---|---|---|---|

| [`ine_poblacion_femicidios.md`](ine_poblacion_femicidios.md) | INE Padrón population by sex/age/nationality (denominators); INE Estadística de Condenados (convictions by crime/sex/nationality, incl. table 28716 sexual crimes by nationality); femicide series back to 1999 | high (official tables, directly cited with table numbers) | Table 28716 (convictions by nationality) referenced but **not yet extracted into CSV** — primary candidate numerator source for T26/T27 |
| [`migracion_espana.md`](migracion_espana.md) | INE migration flows (EMCR/EVR), foreign-nationality & foreign-born stock (Padrón/ECP), Social Security affiliation of foreigners, 2000–2025 — denominator for migrant population by demographic slice | high for Padrón stock figures (exact INE match verified); medium for flow series requiring cross-source joins | **2008 break** (EVR→EMCR methodology change in flow series); **2021 break** (Padrón→ECP stock series); **Brexit reclassification** of UK nationals between EU/non-EU groupings ~2020-2021 |
| [`delegacion_gobierno_femicidio.md`](delegacion_gobierno_femicidio.md) | Official partner/ex-partner femicide registry (LO 1/2004 scope), annual counts 2003–2024 | high (confirmed annual counts, official registry) | Narrow definition — excludes non-partner female homicides, vicarious killings; feminicidio.net's broader definition gives ~2× higher counts; do not mix series without noting scope |
| [`cgpj_violencia_genero.md`](cgpj_violencia_genero.md) | CGPJ judicial statistics — denuncias, protection orders, convictions for partner/ex-partner violence (LO 1/2004 scope) | medium (secondary-summary figures pending verification against annual bulletin PDFs) | Counts court-registered complaints, not police reports or crimes — not directly comparable to MIR; overlaps but doesn't match INE EVDVG (different administrative source) |

| [`homicidio_espana.md`](homicidio_espana.md) | All-cause homicide statistics by sex — INE ECM (cause-of-death), MIR Balance (police-registered), cross-referenced with EIGE/feminicidio.net | medium-high (cross-checked across 2+ official sources for recent years) | INE ECM (deaths-by-confirmed-cause) systematically lower than MIR police counts (e.g. 2023: 300 vs 348) — definitional, not a discrepancy to "resolve"; INE counts may shift year if cause confirmed late |
| [`ine_causas_muerte.md`](ine_causas_muerte.md) | INE Defunciones según la Causa de Muerte (ECM) — full cause-of-death microdata, 2000–2024, sex × 22 age groups × 120 ICD-10 reduced-list causes (~198k cells) | high (official table 7947, pulled via documented JSON API pipeline) | Large table — always validate sums against chapter aggregates (V12-style check) before use; chapter XX = "causas externas" is the relevant bucket for homicide/suicide/accidents |
| [`ine_poblacion_estimaciones.md`](ine_poblacion_estimaciones.md) | INE Estimaciones de Población Actual (table 56934) — population by single-year age × sex, quarterly snapshots 1971–2025; mid-year (1 July) used as mortality-rate denominator | high (2024 female total = 24,881,624 exact INE match, verified) | Some quarters suppressed (`..`) pre-2002; mid-year choice is a deliberate demographic convention (person-years approximation) — don't substitute Jan 1 stock figures without adjusting |

## Quick orientation by question

- **"Where do sexual-violence crime counts come from?"** → `mir_informes_delitos_sexuales.md` (police-recorded) + `fiscalia_memorias.md` (proceedings) + `ine_poblacion_femicidios.md` (convictions, incl. nationality table 28716)
- **"Where's the femicide/homicide data?"** → `delegacion_gobierno_femicidio.md` (partner/ex-partner registry) + `homicidio_espana.md` (all-cause by sex) + `cgpj_violencia_genero.md` (judicial cross-check)
- **"Where's the migrant population denominator?"** → `migracion_espana.md` (flows + stock by nationality/origin/age/sex) + `ine_poblacion_femicidios.md` §1 (Padrón table 31304)
- **"Where's the survey/dark-figure data (vs. police reports)?"** → `macroencuesta.md` + `fuentes_secundarias_analisis_espana.md`
- **"Where do mortality-rate denominators come from?"** → `ine_poblacion_estimaciones.md` (population) + `ine_causas_muerte.md` (deaths by cause)
- **"Where's the migration→hate-speech→aggression pipeline documented?"** → `discurso_odio_inmigracion_espana.md` (official tracking, civil-society monitors, case studies El Ejido/Torre Pacheco)
