Critical Data Caveats

  • Series break 2022: LO 10/2022 (6 Sept 2022) merged "abuso sexual" into "agresión sexual" — pre/post figures are incompatible.
  • Series break pre-2012: MIR data excluded Cataluña, País Vasco, Navarra (~25–30% population) — multiply by ~1.3–1.5 for national estimates.
  • Unresolved B6: Two incompatible rape series in MIR data (Anuario ~5,000/yr vs Informe subcategory ~1,200/yr). Root cause unresolved — blocks rate computation.
  • Survey ≠ Police data: Macroencuesta captures actual prevalence; police data captures reported crimes. Do not use lifetime cumulative / annual reported as a "dark figure multiplier".
  • Reporting rate: ~8% of sexual violence outside partner is reported to police (Macroencuesta 2019). ~92% unreported.
  • Femicide scope: Delegación del Gobierno counts partner/ex-partner homicides only. Total female homicides are higher (INE MNP series).
  • Fiscalía counts proceedings (diligencias previas), not crimes. One crime can generate multiple proceedings — figures are higher than MIR police counts.
  • 2024 Macroencuesta: Methodology changed — NOT directly comparable to 2019. Some observed changes may reflect measurement change, not real change.
  • Foreign victim rate: 4.95× femicide rate ratio (foreign vs Spanish) reflects relative rates per 100k, not absolute numbers. Denominator = female population by nationality (Padrón).
  • Haiku model note: Values from a prior Haiku AI conversation were critically audited. Key corrected figures: femicide 2024 (58→47), total sexual crimes 2024 (21,159→22,846), 2023 total (19,981→21,825).
