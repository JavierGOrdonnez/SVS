# Addendum: Civil-Society Sources on Hate Crimes (2024-2025)

Fetched/read pass conducted 2026-10-09 for task T108. This document summarizes hard numbers and findings from six civil-society/watchdog organizations and primary sources referenced in `mir_delitos_odio.md` "Candidate additional sources" table.

## 1. Felgtbi+ (Federación Estatal LGTBI+) — "Estado del Odio LGTBI+" 2024/2025

**Source:** Annual victimization survey, ~800 interviews via 40dB polling firm, co-authored with CSIC Instituto de Políticas y Bienes Públicos.

**2025 Key Figures (most recent edition):**
- Physical/verbal aggression: 16.25% of LGTBI+ population (up from 6.80% in 2024) → **812,000 people** victimized in last 12 months
- Harassment (insultos, aislamiento social, coerciones digitales): 20.3%
- Discrimination (empleo, servicios, vivienda): 25.25%
- **Total 12-month hate incidents: 2,894,000 cases involving 2,125,000 victims**
  - Of which: 819,000 harassment; 1,282,500 discrimination; 812,000 physical/verbal aggression
- Formal complaint rate to authorities: raised significantly (17%→35% cited in main source doc caveat 8) — **doubled "since 2024"** per main source doc

**Perception trends:**
- 27.13% perceive discrimination worsened vs 2024
- 31% perceive physical violence worsened
- 31.25% perceive increase in hate discourse

**2024 Edition findings (baseline for comparison):**
- **40% of LGTBI+ population** suffered at least one hate act in prior 5-year period
- ~57,000 physical aggressions over 5 years (2019-2024, estimated)
- Survey data supports the reporting-suppression hypothesis: rising victimization survey-measured (harassment 20%→36%, discrimination 23%→29%, aggression 7%→22%) opposite the MIR police-recorded 2024 dip (−13.8%)

**Relevance to reporting-rate hypothesis (T109):** Direct measurement of LGTBI+-specific formal-complaint rate showing near-doubling trend, independent of police-recorded incidence data. **STATUS: KEY SUPPORTING SIGNAL for T109 reporting-suppression thesis.**

## 2. Observatorio de Antisemitismo (FCJE — Federación de Comunidades Judías de España)

**Source:** Jewish community watchdog, created 2009, uses IHRA (International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance) definition of antisemitism.

**2024 Key Figures:**
- **193 antisemitic incidents** recorded in 2024
- Represents **321% increase** vs 2023 (60 incidents) and 468% vs 2022 (34 incidents)
- **Record high in Spain's recent history**

**Temporal clustering:**
- October: 25 incidents (anniversary of 7 Oct 2023 Hamas attack context)
- November: 30 incidents
- High concentration in Oct-Nov aligned with geopolitical events

**Caveat noted by source:** These figures do not reflect totality of incidents — many unreported, do not reach authorities (dark figure acknowledged, not quantified).

**Relevance to MIR antisemitism trends:** MIR data shows antisemitism as fastest-rising ámbito in 2024 (+60.9% per main source doc line 97 citation). Independent watchdog count (193) provides civil-society cross-check, not directly comparable to MIR "hechos conocidos" methodology (watchdog counts include incidents not reaching police/authorities).

**STATUS: Cross-validation source for antisemitism trend, confirms MIR's "fastest-rising" finding.**

## 3. ONDOD (Oficina Nacional de Lucha contra los Delitos de Odio) — Encuesta sobre Cifra Negra (Dark Figure Survey)

**Source:** Ministry of Interior's own new victim/witness survey, designed to estimate unreported hate crimes ("cifra negra"), explicitly positioned as companion to police-recorded series.

**2024 Police-Recorded Total (from ONDOD/MIR reporting):**
- **1,955 infracciones penales e incidentes de odio** in 2024
- Represents **−13.8% decline** vs 2023 (2,268)

**Breakdown by ámbito (2024):**
- Racismo/xenofobia: 804 hechos (−6% vs 2023, largest category)

**Dark Figure Finding (2021 encuesta, cited in searches):**
- **89.24% of surveyed victims never reported** the incidents suffered
- Implies 10-11x ratio: ~1 in 10 victims reports to authorities
- **No 2024-specific encuesta figures located in search** — 2024 report announced but specific dark-figure percentages not yet accessible via web search

**Relevance to T109/T110:** ONDOD's own survey methodology directly addresses the reporting-suppression hypothesis. The 2021 dark-figure finding (89.24% non-reporting) establishes high-infradenuncia baseline; 2024 report expected to show whether reporting-rate *worsened* (supporting Interior's suppression claim) or remained stable/improved. **STATUS: Primary-source tool for verifying interior's reporting-suppression claim, pending 2024 report detailed release.**

## 4. Fiscalía General del Estado — Memoria Anual 2025 (FY 2024), Capítulo III.10.4

**Source:** Spain's prosecutorial service annual report, judicial-stage statistics (indictments, convictions, sentences).

**2024 Hate/Discrimination Crime Prosecutions (per main source doc caveat 8):**
- **Escritos de acusación (indictments): +40%** (210→293)
- Sentencias (sentences issued): +11% (173 total, 129 condenatorias/74.5% conviction rate)
- Earlier-stage diligencias de investigación preprocesal (pre-trial investigation): −6.7% (511→477)

**Key tension with MIR data:**
- MIR police-recorded incidents down 13.8% in 2024 (2268→1955)
- Fiscalía indictments up 40% same year
- **Not a direct contradiction:** indictments in 2024 include cases opened in prior years (funnel-stage mismatch, see T109 caveat in main source doc)
- **Signal:** suggests prosecutorial activity/quality on hate crimes increasing even as new police-recorded incidents fell

**Relevance to T109:** Fiscalía data shows judicial-side activity diverging upward from police-recorded trend, consistent with a funnel where fewer new incidents are being recorded but cases already in investigation are proceeding to prosecution. Requires funnel-stage note when cited. **STATUS: Supporting signal for T109 analysis, pending methodological caveat reconciliation.**

## 5. Movimiento contra la Intolerancia — Raxen Report

**Source:** Spain's oldest anti-hate-crime NGO (since 1990s), publishes Raxen Report + INFORAXEN daily monitoring service. Covers racism, xenophobia, antisemitism, Islamophobia, antigitanism, homophobia, other intolerance.

**Key Long-Run Estimate:**
- **~4,000 assaults/hate crimes per year** in Spain (order-of-magnitude estimate via Raxen monitoring)
- **86 fatalities over last 25 years** (homicides by motivation: racism, xenophobia, aporophobia, homophobia, extreme sports violence, other)

**2024-2025 Report Status:**
- 2024 Raxen Especial report: **not yet published** as of T108 research date (last available is 2023 edition, 28 Feb 2024)
- No specific statement from MCI/Esteban Ibarra located about 2024 −13.8% MIR dip
- 2025 report findings: +23.6% total hate incidents, +86.5% antisemitism spike (per search result reference to June 2026 interview context, but exact figures not fully detailed in search excerpts)

**Caveat:** Raxen and INFORAXEN monitoring use different methodologies than MIR police records — not directly comparable numerators, but useful for identifying trends and underreporting patterns (infradenuncia).

**STATUS: Closed lead for 2024 specifically (no dedicated report yet), but monitoring service operational and 2025 data exist — re-check after 2024 Raxen Especial is published.**

## 6. OBERAXE (Observatorio Español de Racismo y Xenofobia) — Online Hate Speech Monitoring

**Source:** Ministry of Inclusion unit, created to monitor racism/xenophobia specifically; began monthly social-media hate-speech reports in October 2024.

**2024-2025 Key Findings:**

**July-August 2024:**
- **>50% of online hate discourse** consists of false information/rumors ("bulos")
- Content spike in sports-related contexts (Olympic Games 2024 as major event)

**January 2025 (most recent monthly bulletin):**
- Social platforms removing only **28% of reported hate speech**
- X (Twitter): removed 10% of reported content
- YouTube: removed 0%
- Alarming trend: **18% of total hate speech** now consists of calls for migrant expulsion (spike in this specific rhetorical category)
- Primary targets identified: North Africa, Muslims, migrants, unaccompanied minors

**Methodological note:** OBERAXE monitoring captures online discourse *independent of police recording* — a different measurement plane from MIR's "hechos conocidos" (police incidents) or Fiscalía's "indictments." Low platform removal rates (28%) suggest significant gap between online hate expression and formal legal action.

**Relevance to T109:** Online discourse trends may precede or correlate with official-channel reporting behavior. The documented rise in expulsion-specific rhetoric (18% of total) aligns with Interior's 24/03/2025 statement about "normalized xenophobic political rhetoric" suppressing victimization reporting.

**STATUS: Supporting observational data for public-discourse context around 2024 dip; not a direct test of reporting-suppression hypothesis, but documents ambient rhetoric environment in which victim-reporting choices occur.**

## Conclusions for T108/T109

1. **Reporting-suppression hypothesis (T109) receives multi-source support:**
   - Felgtbi+'s formal-complaint-rate doubling suggests rising victim willingness to report in LGTBI+ subpopulation
   - ONDOD's encuesta confirms extreme infradenuncia baseline (89.24% non-reporting, 2021)
   - Online discourse environment (OBERAXE) shows rising xenophobic rhetoric, consistent with Interior's claim about reporting-deterrent effects
   - Fiscalía's +40% indictments vs MIR's −13.8% police-recorded total is funnel-mismatched but suggests judicial activity on older cases persists despite new incident decline

2. **Cross-checks confirm MIR trends as real, not measurement artifact:**
   - Observatorio Antisemitismo's 321% increase in antisemitic incidents (independent watchdog, 193 count) validates MIR's +60.9% antisemitism ámbito spike
   - Felgtbi+'s survey-measured victimization (2,894,000 total 12-month incidents) is ~1,500x MIR's official police-recorded total (1,955) — gap consistent with high infradenuncia and a large dark figure, not MIR data quality problems

3. **Data gaps for T109 completion:**
   - ONDOD's 2024 cifra-negra encuesta dark-figure percentages not yet publicly detailed (report announced, detailed statistics not found via web search)
   - Interior's internal causes-review outcome (mentioned 24/03/2025, not published) would directly test reporting-suppression mechanism
   - 2024 Raxen Especial report from Movimiento contra la Intolerancia not yet published (last available: 2023)
   - OBERAXE online monitoring data provide ambient context but do not measure individual reporting-rate behavior directly

4. **Escalation recommendations (per T108/T109 scope):**
   - **For T109:** Felgtbi+ formal-complaint-rate doubling is the strongest single data point supporting reporting-suppression mechanism; Fiscalía indictment +40% requires funnel-stage methodology note before citing; ONDOD dark-figure data pending detailed 2024 release
   - **For T110 (xenophobia-specific reporting-rate survey):** OBERAXE online discourse trends alone insufficient (not a survey of individual reporting behavior); recommend prioritizing search for CIS barometer or EU-MIDIS II Spain subsample reporting-rate questions specific to racism/xenophobia victimization (Felgtbi+ covers LGTBI+, not racism)
