## Caveats

- CGPJ denuncias counts court-registered complaints, not police reports
- Scope limited to LO 1/2004 (partner/ex-partner); domestic violence between other family members is in a separate series
- INE EVDVG covers similar scope but from a different administrative source — counts may differ slightly
