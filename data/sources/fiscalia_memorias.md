# Source: Fiscalía General del Estado — Memorias Anuales

**Publisher:** Fiscalía General del Estado  
**Series:** Annual Memoria — covers diligencias previas (preliminary proceedings) initiated  
**Timing:** Published each September, covering the prior calendar year  
**Note:** Fiscalía figures count *proceedings opened*, not crimes or victims — typically higher than MIR police counts because one crime can generate multiple proceedings.

## Key figures

### 2024 (Memoria 2025, presented 5 September 2025)
- Diligencias previas for sexual assault (agresiones sexuales): **20,711** (+12.11% vs 2023)
- Total diligencias for crimes against sexual liberty: **32,357** (+9.96%)
- Growth is moderating after the large post-LO10/2022 surge

### 2023 (Memoria 2024)
- Diligencias previas for sexual assault: **18,474** (+92% vs 2022 — due to LO 10/2022 reclassification)
- "Alarming spiral" language used by Fiscalía

### 2022 (Memoria 2023)
- Diligencias previas: **9,603** (pre-LO10/2022 definition)
- Jump to 18,474 in 2023 is a definitional break, not a real doubling

## Caveats

- Fiscalía counts proceedings, not crimes. One crime may appear in multiple proceedings.
- The +92% jump from 2022→2023 is driven by reclassification: "abuso sexual" now counts as "agresión sexual" — not a real surge of that magnitude.
- Compare with MIR crime counts (primary police record) and INE Condenados (judicial outcomes) to triangulate.
